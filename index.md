# Alex Kucera

**Economics PhD Student · Michigan State University**

I am a PhD student working on the economics of crime. My research examines illicit drug markets, using data from wastewater monitoring programs. I am particularly interested in how economic theory can be used to explain behavior in these markets.

I am currently a 5th year PhD Student at Michigan State University.

## Research

My current projects focus on:

- **Demand in Illicit Drug Markets:** I use data from wastewater monitoring programs to directly measure demand responses to shocks in illicit drug markets.
- **Illicit Drug Network Analysis:** Understanding how these networks are structured and how the features of nodes provides greater insight into how interventions may be successes or failures
